Stop the Scammers: OTP Security Guide

Your OTP is the key to your digital vault. Keep it safe.

How OTP Fraud Happens

Cybercriminals use psychological tricks to steal your One-Time Password.

The Fake Call

Scammers pose as bank staff or "support" and ask for your OTP to "verify" your account.

Malicious Links

You receive an SMS with a link to a fake "KYC update" page that steals your code.

Remote Access

They trick you into installing screen-sharing apps like AnyDesk to see your messages.

How to Prevent It

  • ✅ Never share your OTP with anyone, even bank staff.
  • ✅ Read the OTP message carefully to see what it's for.
  • ✅ Never click on links in suspicious SMS messages.
  • ✅ Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) via Auth Apps.
  • ✅ Report suspicious calls to your bank immediately.
⚠️ REMEMBER: A legitimate bank will NEVER ask for your OTP.

In Case of Fraud?

If you have shared your OTP accidentally:

  1. Call your bank's official helpline to block your card.
  2. Report the crime at cybercrime.gov.in (or your local authority).
  3. Change all your passwords immediately.
