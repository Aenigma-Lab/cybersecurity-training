How to Get Hacked - Phishing Awareness

▼

Hackers use various methods to gain access to your WhatsApp account:

  • Phishing Links: Fake links that look legitimate but steal your credentials
  • Verification Code Scam: Attackers trick you into sharing the 6-digit verification code
  • Malware: Malicious software hidden in shared files or apps
  • SIM Swapping: Criminals transfer your number to their SIM card
💡 Pro Tip: WhatsApp will NEVER ask for your verification code. Never share it with anyone, not even friends or family!
🛡️

Prevent Fraud & Scams

▼

Stay alert against these common scams:

  • Urgency Scams: "Emergency" requests demanding immediate money transfer
  • Impersonation: Scammers pretending to be friends, family, or officials
  • Fake Job Offers: Too-good-to-be-true job opportunities requiring payment
  • Lottery Winnings: Messages claiming you've won prizes you never entered
💡 Always Verify: Call your friend on their known number to confirm before sending money, even if the message seems urgent!

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

▼

What is 2FA? An extra layer of security that requires a PIN when registering your phone number with WhatsApp.

Steps to enable:

  1. Open WhatsApp Settings
  2. Tap Account → Two-step verification
  3. Enter a 6-digit PIN
  4. Add an optional email for recovery
💡 Remember: Choose a PIN you'll remember but others won't guess. Avoid simple numbers like 123456 or your birth date!
📥

Disable Auto Media Download

▼

Auto-downloading media can expose you to malicious files. Here's how to disable it:

  1. Open WhatsApp Settings
  2. Go to Storage and Data
  3. Tap "When using mobile data"
  4. Turn OFF photos, videos, audio, and documents
💡 Better Safe: Manually download files only when you trust the sender. This prevents automatic malware execution!
📱

Unknown App Installation Risks

▼

Installing apps from unofficial sources is dangerous:

  • Modded Apps: Fake WhatsApp versions with hidden spyware
  • APK Files: Android packages that may contain malware
  • Temporary Numbers: Apps requiring phone verification can steal your account
💡 Official Sources Only: Always download apps from Google Play Store (Android) or Apple App Store (iOS). Check developer names and reviews!
📞

Handle Unknown Calls & Messages

▼

Protect yourself from suspicious contacts:

  • Don't Answer: Ignore calls from unknown international numbers
  • Block & Report: Use WhatsApp's built-in block and report features
  • Privacy Settings: Limit who can see your profile photo and status
  • Group Invites: Control who can add you to groups
💡 Privacy First: Go to Settings → Account → Privacy and set "Last Seen", "Profile Photo", and "About" to "My Contacts" or "Nobody"!
