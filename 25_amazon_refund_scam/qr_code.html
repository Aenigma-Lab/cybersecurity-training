🚨 Forensic Lab: The "Quishing" Attack

🚩 FLAG 1: THE "RECEIVE" SCAM. In UPI, you never need to scan a QR code or enter your PIN to *receive* money. If you scan a code to get a refund, you are actually authorizing a Payment *to* the scammer.

🚩 FLAG 2: URGENCY & SAFETY. Scammers use terms like "Secure Gateway" or "Verify Identity" to make the fraud sound like a security feature.

🚩 FLAG 3: OFF-PLATFORM. Amazon processes refunds automatically to your original payment method. They will never ask you to use a separate QR code or a third-party UPI app.
