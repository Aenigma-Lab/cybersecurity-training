🔐 SIM Swapping

What is it?

Scammers trick your phone company into moving your number to their SIM card. Then they get your texts and calls.

How to Spot It

  • 📱 No cell service suddenly
  • ✉️ Can't receive texts
  • 🔓 Accounts you didn't access

How to Protect Yourself

  • Set a PIN with your phone carrier
  • Use an authenticator app (not SMS)
  • Strong unique passwords everywhere
  • Don't overshare personal info online

What a SIM PIN Protects

A SIM PIN (set on your phone's settings) protects the physical chip.

If your phone is stolen: The thief cannot take your SIM card and put it into their own phone to get your calls or texts because their device will demand the PIN.

If your phone restarts: The SIM won't connect to the network until the PIN is entered.

How to Set a SIM PIN

The process varies slightly depending on your device's operating system:

For iPhone/iPad: Go to Settings > Cellular > SIM PIN. Toggle the switch on and enter your carrier's default PIN.

For Android (General): Open Settings > Security > More security settings > SIM card lock. Toggle Lock SIM card to "On" and enter the default PIN.

For Samsung Galaxy: Navigate to Settings > Security and privacy > More security settings > SIM card security. Toggle Lock SIM card on.

⚠️ Act Fast: If attacked, call your carrier immediately and change all passwords!
