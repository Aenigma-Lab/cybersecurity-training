🎯 Email Domain Fraud

How Hackers Trick You

Hackers use look-alike domains to make fake emails look real.

Real: company.com
Fake: c0mpany.com (zero instead of letter O)

💡 How to Protect Yourself

  1. Always check the domain - look at the part after @
  2. Hover over links before clicking
  3. Verify requests through another channel (call the person)
  4. Look for typos - 0 vs O, 1 vs l, rn vs m
